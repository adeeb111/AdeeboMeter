# Code of conduct

AdeeboMeter is made for people who are fixing a slow connection, learning how networks work, or helping someone else
with theirs. Everyone taking part - in issues, discussions, pull requests, translations - is treated with respect.

**Expected:** be kind and patient (many people here are not network engineers, and that's the point), assume good
faith, give feedback about the work rather than the person, and credit other people's help.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults or personal attacks; discrimination of any kind; posting someone else's private
information (addresses, phone numbers, network details from their screenshots); sexual language or imagery; spam.

**If something happens:** open an issue, or, if it's about a person, use the "Report content" option on the comment
or contact the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may edit or remove comments,
close threads, or block anyone who doesn't follow this, for as long as needed.

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
