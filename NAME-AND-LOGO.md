# The AdeeboMeter name and logo

The code is free under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE). The **name "AdeeboMeter"** and **its logo** are not part of that
license: they belong to Adeeb Khan (AdeeboMeter™), so people always know which copy is the real one.

## You can, without asking

- Say your project is "based on AdeeboMeter" or "a fork of AdeeboMeter", in plain text.
- Write about AdeeboMeter, review it, make videos and tutorials, and show its screens.
- Link to adeebometer.com and use the name to point people to the official app.

## You can't

- Ship a changed or rebuilt copy called AdeeboMeter, or with a name that sounds like it (e.g. "AdeeboMeter Pro").
- Use the logo, or one that looks like it, for your copy.
- Make your copy, its website or its store page look like it comes from Adeeb Khan or adeebometer.com.

A fork keeps the GPL and the copyright notice, and gets its own name and logo. Changing them takes a few minutes:
the window title, the tray name, `Program.Tagline`, `assets/AdeeboMeter.ico` and the logo in `docs/`.

## Not in this repository

The Hall of Fame signing key stays with Adeeb Khan. A build made from this code works fully, but its award cards
say "unofficial build" — that's how the real cards stay real.
