Streaming

Where the video comes from - CDNs

A copy of the show in a building near you.

Big services copy popular videos to servers inside or near your provider's network (a content delivery network), so a stream rarely crosses the whole internet.

How the conversation goes

Your PCService

  1. Your PC to Service: play this show

  2. Service to Your PC: get it from the server in your city

  3. Your PC → Nearby server: chunk 1

  4. Nearby server → Your PC: chunk 1

When it goes wrong

When the nearby copy is busy or down, the player is sent further away - the stream server's delay jumps while your own line is fine.
