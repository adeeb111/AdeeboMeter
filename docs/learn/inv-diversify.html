Spreading the risk

One company can sink; a basket rarely does.

Holding many companies (an index fund holds hundreds) means one bad company barely dents the whole. Spreading across countries and types of investment smooths things further.

When it goes wrong

Owning five tech companies isn't spread - they tend to fall together.
