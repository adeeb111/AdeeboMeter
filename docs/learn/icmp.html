How the conversation goes

Your PCRouter

  1. Your PC to Router: echo request (TTL 1)

  2. Router to Your PC: time exceeded - I'm hop 1

  3. Your PC → Server: echo request

  4. Server → Your PC: echo reply - 22 ms

When it goes wrong

No ping reply doesn't always mean down - many servers ignore ping on purpose.

To capture ping and traceroute results together, create a packet loss report for your internet provider.
