TLS - the padlock

Proves who the server is, then encrypts everything.

The padlock: proves the server is real and encrypts everything after.

How the conversation goes

Your PCServer

  1. Your PC to Server: ClientHello - I speak TLS 1.3

  2. Server to Your PC: ServerHello + certificate

  3. Your PC to Server: Finished - keys agreed

  4. Your PC to Server: encrypted request

  5. Server to Your PC: encrypted answer

When it goes wrong

A certificate error means the wrong server, a wrong clock, or something in the middle.
