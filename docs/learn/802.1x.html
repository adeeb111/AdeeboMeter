How the internet talks

802.1X - company sign-in for Wi-Fi and network ports

The network checks who you are before letting you in.

Work networks check who you are (certificate or password) before letting you on.

How the conversation goes

Your PCSwitch / AP

  1. Your PC to Switch / AP: EAPOL start

  2. Switch / AP → RADIUS server: here's who it claims to be

  3. RADIUS server → Switch / AP: Access-Accept, VLAN 20

  4. Switch / AP to Your PC: you're in

When it goes wrong

Can't join the work Wi-Fi but others can: your certificate or account, not the radio.
