IPTV and live channels

Live TV over the internet - less buffer, less forgiveness.

A live channel can't download far ahead - the next minute hasn't happened yet - so it keeps only a few seconds in the buffer.

When it goes wrong

Many stutters on a live channel come from the channel's own server; when your router and provider look fine, the record says "the stream's server".
