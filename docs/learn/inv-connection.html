What your connection means for trading

The network carries the order; it doesn't fill it.

Your order travels from your PC through Wi-Fi, your router and your provider to the broker. AdeeboMeter times that path every few seconds while the market is open and says whose side a slow moment is on.

How the conversation goes

YouRouter

  1. You to Router: Buy (2 ms)

  2. Router → Provider: Buy (15 ms)

  3. Provider → Broker: Buy (12 ms)

  4. Broker → You: order received

When it goes wrong

If the order screen spins after a lost reply, check your orders list before clicking again - a second click can mean a second order.
