How Adeebo Points work

Adeebo Points are a small reward for learning AdeeboMeter and how networks work. They're on from the start; you can switch them off in Settings › Adeebo Points (your total is kept). Every item pays once. Points are kept on your PC only - nothing is sent anywhere, there are no streaks and no reminders. Press the Adeebo button at the top of AdeeboMeter to see your rank, your progress on each path, and every feature with a Go that takes you straight there.

Learn, then check yourself

Learn a topic

Open Learn and pick a topic. Each one is two lines and a picture. Scroll to the end and stay at least 15 seconds: +2. Many topics also have See it live on my PC, which records 10 seconds of that protocol on your own PC (needs Wireshark).

Check yourself

Every topic ends with one question and four answers. Right the first time: +2. Wrong: the right answer is shown, and that question pays nothing - not now, and not after a restart. That way guessing doesn't pay.

Check your progress

The Adeebo button at the top shows your total, your rank, how far you are from the next one, each learning path's progress, and every feature with ✓ when it's done. Search it (“mtr”, “dns”, “wifi”) and press Go.

Join the Hall of Fame

Reached 500? Here's how to get your name on this page.

  1. Make your card. On the Adeebo page, press Make my award card and type your name the way you want it shown. AdeeboMeter saves the picture to your Pictures folder and copies its text (name, points, date and claim code) so you can paste it.
  2. Share it anywhere you like - LinkedIn, X, a group chat. It's your picture.
  3. Send it here, one of two ways:
    Send it with the GitHub form Email it

    With the GitHub form it's automatic: paste what AdeeboMeter copied, drag in the picture, and a check runs straight away. If the code matches, you're on this page within a couple of minutes. By email, paste the same text and attach the picture. You'll be added by hand, usually within a few days.

How a card is checked

Each card has a claim code like ADB-7K3F-Q9XM-2PLD-…. AdeeboMeter makes it from your name, your points and the date, using a key that only the official download contains. The Hall of Fame computes the same code. A card edited in a picture editor, or someone else's card with a new name, doesn't match. AdeeboMeter built from the source code has no key, so its cards say “unofficial build”.

What the code proves, honestly: that the card came out of AdeeboMeter unchanged - not that every point was earned. AdeeboMeter is offline and open source and your points live on your PC, so a determined person could still cheat. AdeeboMeter only counts items that really exist (never more than everything there is to earn), the check turns away impossible totals and second entries for the same name, and any entry can be taken down. It's a celebration, not a certificate.

Questions

Does AdeeboMeter send my points anywhere?

No. AdeeboMeter never sends anything about your points. It only saves a picture, and you decide whether to share it.

What goes on this page?

The name you typed on the card, your points, the date and the claim code, plus a link to your picture if you sent one with the GitHub form. Nothing else. Pictures aren't loaded into this page; they're links to GitHub.

Can I be taken off?

Yes. Open an issue or send an email and you'll be removed.

My code doesn't match.

Type the name, points and date exactly as they are on the card, or paste the text AdeeboMeter copied. If you've earned more points since, make the card again and send the new one.

I'm under 500 - where am I?

Press the Adeebo button in AdeeboMeter. The bar shows how far you are from the next rank, and each learning path shows what's left.
