Try a two-minute experiment

  1. Open AdeeboLab and choose the Gamer example. The provider equipment is an illustration, not discovered hardware.
  2. Choose Break it, then Wrong gateway. Keep the same starting device and destination.
  3. Press Play. Use Step or Step back to follow the explanation at your own pace.
  4. Try DNS times out next. Notice how a name lookup failure differs from a packet that cannot leave the PC.

What the experiment shows

  • The packet cannot leave the PCStart with the local path and gateway settings in this simulation.
  • Names stop answeringDNS can fail while other parts of a connection still work.
  • The real connection is slowUse a real measurement. A simulated fault does not diagnose your PC, Wi-Fi or provider.
Does AdeeboLab test my real connection?

No. AdeeboLab simulates packets on a drawing. It sends no real network traffic. Use AdeeboMeter for measurements from your Windows PC.

Do I need to install AdeeboLab?

No. Open the simulator in your browser. Its labels and explanations are currently in English.
