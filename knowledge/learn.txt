== tcp
title: TCP - the reliable conversation
kind: protocol
tag: Numbered, acknowledged, resent when lost.
check: What does TCP do when a packet goes missing? | Sends it again, so nothing arrives missing | Skips it to stay fast | Closes the connection | Asks DNS again

== udp
title: UDP - fast and fire-and-forget
