Severity: crash
Where: src/Points.cs:43-55 (Load), :38 (Points.Me); callers FullView.Points.cs:98 (SyncPointsBtn), FullView.cs:391 (ShowTab2), Settings.cs:104

### 4. "Describe it in your own words" sends ordinary sentences to the wrong guide
Severity: wrong result
Where: src/Ui/FullView.Overview.cs FixWords and FixMatch (`" " + wd` prefix match; rule 8 `"vpn|work|office|company"`)
How to reproduce and what happens (ran against the test build):

| Typed | Result |
|---|---|
| "my internet doesn't work" | guide:vpn, "Sounds like it works at home but not on VPN or at work" |
| "printer not working" | guide:vpn |
| "my hotspot doesn't work" | guide:vpn |
