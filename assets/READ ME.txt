AdeeboMeter __VERSION__  -  is it me or the network?
No cloud. No telemetry. Never listens. Every packet it sends is listed in About - and you can switch each one off.

Install (about ten seconds, no administrator rights)
  Double-click AdeeboMeter.exe and choose Install.
  Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" - the app isn't code-signed. Choose More info > Run anyway.
  It installs just for you, adds itself to the Start menu and starts in the bottom-right corner of your screen.

Use it
  Click the tray icon or press Ctrl+Alt+A for the full story. Esc goes back to the corner.

Update
  Double-click the new AdeeboMeter.exe and choose Update. Your settings come along.

Remove
  Settings > Apps > AdeeboMeter > Uninstall, or right-click the tray icon > Uninstall AdeeboMeter.

Rather not install?
  Choose "Just run it from here" - it runs from this folder and keeps its settings next to it.

How it works
  AdeeboMeter - Theory of Everything.pdf, in this folder.
  The source, the build and the website: https://github.com/adeeb111/AdeeboMeter
